Source tablet: Teclast T65 Max A8D3 hardware running the official A8D4 ROW
Android 15 stack `V1.07_20250603` on slot A.

  `work/device-captures/a8d4-rooted/runtime-vendor-files.txt`

The runtime expansion is generated by `lineage/tools/merge-runtime-blobs.py`.
It adds stock files that were actually mapped by live processes but were not
in the reference-derived seed. `lineage/tools/analyze-elf-dependencies.py`
then computes iterative `DT_NEEDED` closure against the rooted vendor, ODM,
product and system_ext inventories. It added 40 unambiguous stock libraries.
The final pass inspected 926 ELF files and found zero further stock additions,
zero ambiguous matches and zero objdump failures. The 128 remaining SONAMEs
are system/APEX dependencies and remain an explicit build-time compatibility
check.

The 2,924 broad candidates are deliberately not copied wholesale. Most are
generic libraries or duplicate camera tuning variants and still require
hardware-specific justification.

Files with `private` in their names and complete bugreports may contain
incidental identifiers. Do not publish them without redaction.

The shareable `kernel.txt` capture deliberately removes Android bootconfig
keys `serialno`, `sn1`, `sn2`, `imei`, `meid`, and `socid`. The shareable
runtime DTS replaces the live `serialno` property with `<redacted>`.
